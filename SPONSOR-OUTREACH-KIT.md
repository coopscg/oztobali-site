# OztoBali Sponsor Outreach Kit

Goal: land 15-20 paying sponsors at ~$100-150/month (or discounted 3-6 month prepay) by Christmas 2026 = $2,000/month run-rate.

This is a numbers game. Expect roughly a 5-10% reply rate and a 1-3% close rate on cold outreach to a brand-new, unproven site — so budget for contacting 150-300 businesses to land 15-20 sponsors. Track everything in a simple spreadsheet: Company | Contact method | Date sent | Follow-up date | Status | Tier interested in.

Point every prospect to **oztobali.com/partner** — always send that link, it does a lot of the selling for you.

## What to do with replies

- **"Yes, interested"** → send them the /partner page, confirm tier, invoice via whatever you use (Stripe/PayPal invoice is fine for this scale), then add them to the relevant data file (`data/developers.ts` for property, or a new sponsor section) and ask Claude to wire in their listing.
- **"Maybe, tell me more about traffic"** → be honest about where the site is right now (new, growing) — offer a discounted "founding sponsor" rate (e.g. 50% off for 3 months) in exchange for being an early, visible sponsor plus a testimonial once you have real numbers.
- **No reply after 1 week** → one polite follow-up, then move on. Don't chase past that.
